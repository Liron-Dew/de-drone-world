📌 PROJECT: De Drone World — Cinematic Aerial Intelligence
   Revamp & Redesign of https://thedroneworld.in/

🎯 MISSION:
   We are revamping the original De Drone World website with a
   modern, cinematic UI featuring dark theme, neon cyan (#00d4ff)
   accents, Orbitron headings, and smooth scroll animations.

🏢 ABOUT THE COMPANY:
   - DGCA-approved RPTO (Remote Pilot Training Organisation)
   - Based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India
   - Founded by aviation experts from the Indian Armed Forces
   - Registered as "De Drone World Solutions Pvt Ltd"
   - Provides drone pilot training, aerial services, and manufacturing

🎨 DESIGN SYSTEM:
   - Background: #0a0a0a (near black)
   - Accent: #00d4ff (neon cyan) with glow effects
   - Surface: #111111, #1a1a1a
   - Fonts: Orbitron (headings), Inter (body)
   - Buttons: neon-btn (solid cyan), neon-btn-ghost (outlined)
   - Animations: fade-up on scroll (ai-in/ai-visible), particles, glow
   - Section style: clip-path angled edges, grid texture overlay
